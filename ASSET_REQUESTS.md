# Claybound — optional art replacements

The connected mechanics already work with sculpted clay stand-ins. The windmill and forest mushrooms now use supplied models; the golden seed and leafy vine are sculpted in the scene. These two remaining custom models would add the most visual character. Use the new clay ball's fine fingerprints and cracks, with rounded edges and a matte surface. Supply self-contained GLB files with embedded textures; separate animated parts are welcome.

| Asset | Used in | Shape and animation | Runtime fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cavern pulse beacon | The Two-Beat Gallery, The Lantern Climb and Mountain Heart | Slate clay drum or lantern with a cream ring and turquoise core | About 1.8 units tall. Origin at base centre; separate core/ring allows the game to signal bridge phases. Optional pulse clip. |
| City counterweight bridge | Counterweight Court, Gondola Exchange and The Sky Bell | Orange clay deck, blue support, cream hanging weights and ropes | Centre pivot at the walkable top; deck nominally 5 units wide × 1.9 deep. Separate deck and weights. The game drives the tilt and raises the linked lift, so no baked travel animation is needed. |

The current blockout art is in `dist/setpieces.js`. These models replace visible props; platform collision, wind force, timers and safe landings remain controlled by the game. No missing model URLs are shipped.

## The Soft Dream (chapter five)

Every creature, statue and piece of furniture in the dream ships as a sculpted clay stand-in built in `dist/dream/*.js`; the collision is always the platform data, so a supplied model only replaces what is seen. The Upside-Down Orchard's two dome islands and its five rope-hung saucers were the first to use supplied models — the clay planets and frosted bowls in `dist/assets/dream-*.glb`, prepared by `scripts/prepare-dream-assets.py` and placed by `dist/dream-assets.js` so each planet's core orb is the dome's collider. The orchard's canopy is the next: twelve copies of one supplied abstract sculpture (`dist/assets/dream-sculpture.glb`), hung along the canopy curve as modelled and each turned and sized a little differently, are the roof the ropes rise into. The Melted Parade's hat stack is the third: one supplied clay hat (`dist/assets/dream-hat.glb`), cloned at five brim widths onto the hat-worm's plinth and tumbled by the same keyframes the sculpted hats had. It arrived at 324,212 triangles and is the one model shipped decimated — welded and simplified with glTF Transform to about 8,100 before the repack, with its metallic-roughness map left out so it stays as matte as the rest of the clay; the manifest records the adaptation and `tests/dream-models.mjs` holds the count under 12,000. The two parade creatures came next: the `hatworm` is the supplied caterpillar (`dist/assets/dream-caterpillar.glb`, uploaded unrigged and boned at load by `dist/dream-rigs.js` — seven stations along the body, the walk a hump travelling down them) wearing three of the supplied hat, and the giraffe is the supplied one (`dist/assets/dream-giraffe.glb`, uploaded with its vendor rig, no clips), posed at load so the level's decks are its body — back, the crook of its neck, the top of its head — asleep until the hat-worm's pull wakes the parade, then walking off. Both keep their painted colours, as the planets do. The orchard's great inverted tree grew its crown last: four copies of one supplied iced-apple sculpture (`dist/assets/dream-fruit.glb`, 10,573 triangles, shipped as uploaded), hung by their stems under the cream trunk's foot at four widths, each turned a little; the leaves and the hanging fruit under the canopy stay sculpted clay (the leaf is the forest drifter's clay leaf), and clay iced apples stand in for the crown without the model. All of the rest are nice-to-have. Colours live in the chapter's palette slots, so supply neutral (cream) albedo where a colour is not named and let the material do the rest; matte clay surfaces, rounded edges. A prop a section stacks or scatters is best supplied under 15,000 triangles, or it will be decimated the same way.

**Supplied:** the Crooked Garden's **watching flower** (`clay+flower+model.glb`, a Tripo export: pink petals around a cream eyeball, green stem, two leaves, a root blob, 1 unit tall, no rig, no pupil) ships as `dist/assets/dream-flower.glb` via `scripts/prepare-dream-assets.py`, listed in `dist/assets/dream-assets.json` with the other dream models. `dist/dream-assets.js` rigs it at load — the mesh is cut into head and stem so the head can turn, the eyeball is located on the head and a glossy pupil and a petal-pink lid are added — and the garden slides the pupil toward the player, turns the head, blinks the lid and leans the stem.

**Supplied:** the Melted Parade's **carnival dressing** — four Tripo exports from `new assets/level-5-surreal-psychadelic/parade 3d assets/`, each one textured mesh of about 10,000 triangles, shipped as `dist/assets/dream-banner.glb`, `dream-column.glb`, `dream-cane.glb` and `dream-sun.glb` by the same script. The **decorative banner** (swag valance with gold buttons over a hanging smiley-flower pennant) is cut in two at load in `dist/dream-assets.js`: the valance runs along the hat-worm bridge's underside, gathered while the worm is coiled and stretched with the pull, and the pennant hangs on the plinth. The **candy column** is the stick of the spiral-sun lollipop, its bow and medallion at the disc's centre; its beaded pedestal is cut away and the stick is carried on down to the ground as a sculpted cream cylinder with the candy stripe wound on. The **candy-cane post** stands twice as banner towers at the edges of the bridge's frame and three times, small and hazed, as far spires; the **smiling sun** hangs in the sky behind. With these the parade brings its own horizon (`quietBackdrop`) and the placeholder columns sink while the player is in it.

**Supplied:** the Crooked Garden's **gate** (`Meshy_AI_Clay_Garden_Arch_0917232650_texture.glb`, a Meshy export uploaded to `~/Downloads`: a salmon clay arch on a base, a vine climbing its left leg to a pink flower with a bare cream eyeball, a green curl hanging in the bow; one mesh, 20,444 triangles, 13 MB of PNG textures) ships as `dist/assets/dream-arch.glb` — 1.26 MB after the repack, its metallic-roughness map left out like the hat's. `dist/dream-assets.js` fits the eyeball at load (a sphere through the mesh around a seed read off the placed model) and sets the flowers' pupil and lid on it; `dist/dream/garden.js` stands the gate on the arch deck, shifted so its off-centre opening is the doorway the flag stands in, and registers the eye with the flowers' `watch()`, so it follows the player and blinks. The head is part of the arch, so it does not turn as the free flowers' heads do. Without the model the sculpted arch stands in.

**Supplied:** The Breathing Corridor's **eye** (`Meshy_AI_Fleshy_Eyeball_0918110855_texture.glb`, a Meshy export uploaded to `~/Downloads` like the arch: one mesh, 3,003 triangles — the lightest model in the chapter — a near-perfect ball of radius .9314 with fleshy relief and fine red capillaries, 16.5 MB of PNG textures of which a 4096² metallic-roughness map is over half) ships as `dist/assets/dream-eyeball.glb` — 610 KB after the repack, its metallic-roughness map left out like the hat's and the arch's. It is the one supplied eye whose **pupil is painted into the colour map** rather than set on it at load: a hard dark disc about 26° of arc across, 44% of the ball's width, sitting 4.6° off the model's own +z, which is left as painted. So nothing is rigged onto this one — the **ball itself turns**. `EYEBALL` in `dist/dream-assets.js` records the fitted sphere, and `dreamEyeball` hands back the ball on a gaze group; `dist/dream/corridor.js` slerps that group toward the player inside a cone of 23.6°, well inside the 68° of turn that would bring the second dark cap the bake mirrored onto the ball's back round to the silhouette (`tests/dream-models.mjs` holds the two figures against each other). The corridor recesses the ball into a socket of rolled folds — a dark-red backing buried in the stripes, a vermilion lower lip and a deep-red upper lip that bow forward and cut across the ball so what shows is an almond of about 70% of it, and a magenta brow over them — each fold a `ribbon` sweeping on past the eye across the strata it lies over, the way the painting's folds do. The two lips slide together to blink. Without the model the sculpted cream ball and dark pupil stand in, in the same socket.
